Frequently Asked Questions

How does shipping and delivery work?

All orders are securely packaged and dispatched promptly via nationwide courier. Standard delivery is R150.00. Delivery is completely FREE whenever your total order subtotal after any applied discounts is R2500.00 or more!

How does the free delivery threshold interact with the 10% multi-buy discount?

Free delivery requires a net order value of R2500.00 or more after discounts. For example, if your initial cart subtotal is R2500.00 and a 10% multi-buy discount reduces it to R2250.00, the net subtotal is under R2500.00, so standard R150.00 shipping applies.

Do I need an account to place an order?

No account creation required! Simply fill out your name, contact phone, email, and delivery address at checkout. We reserve your items and email you an official invoice with stock confirmation and banking details.

How can I contact support?

You can call or WhatsApp us directly on 068 636 2665 or email us at cheapcheapza@gmail.com.
