Frequently Asked Questions

How does shipping and delivery work?

All orders are securely packaged and dispatched promptly via reliable nationwide courier services. Standard nationwide shipping is R150.00, and delivery is completely FREE on all orders of R2500.00 or more!

Does the multi-buy discount apply automatically?

Yes! When you add 2 or more items to your cart, our system automatically calculates and applies a 10% discount to your items subtotal.

How do I make a payment?

Once you place an order request, we confirm stock availability and send an official invoice with payment and banking details directly to your email.
